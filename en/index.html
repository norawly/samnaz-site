Process

Five stages
from enquiry to handover

Scope, schedule and price are fixed before any machine reaches the site. Stages advance on their own — or click the one you need.

01

Enquiry and brief

We break down the task, review the drawings and clarify site and access conditions. By the end of this stage it is clear which machines and crews are needed.

What we need from youa brief or design drawings
What we clarifyvolumes, region, schedule, site access
Turnaround1–2 business days
02

Estimate and pricing

We calculate volumes, select the machines and crews, and factor in logistics and mobilisation. You receive an itemised estimate — with no vague “other works” lines.

What you receivean estimate itemised by volume
Also includeda works schedule
Turnaround3–7 business days
03

Contract and mobilisation

We fix the schedule, the responsibilities of each side and the payment terms, then move machines and people to the site — anywhere in Kazakhstan.

Paperworkworks contract, annexes, schedule
Mobilisationmachines and crews on site
Turnaroundfrom 5 days depending on the region
04

Execution

We work to the approved schedule, with survey control, adherence to levels and tolerances, and regular reporting to the client on completed volumes.

Controlsurvey, levels, tolerances
Reportingagainst completed volumes
Servicingon-site machine maintenance
05

Handover and warranty

We prepare as-built documentation, hand over the volumes and sign off the acts. After handover we stay in touch — the project keeps warranty support.

Paperworkas-built documentation, sign-off acts
After handoverwarranty support
Outcomea project accepted by the client
