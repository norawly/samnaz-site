About us

A dependable partner
on projects of any scale

SAMNAZ ENGINEERING has worked in Kazakhstan's construction market since 2003. Over that time we have established ourselves as a dependable partner that consistently delivers on what it commits to.

With experience on construction projects of national scale, we have proven our professionalism and our ability to deliver complex engineering tasks quickly.

Alongside construction services, the company rents out its own machines — from excavators and bulldozers to graders, rollers and dump trucks.

The works schedule is fixed in the contract. Our own machines and permanent crews let us hold the pace even when volumes change.

History

Twenty years
on the country's map

Every project delivered alongside an international main contractor. Pick a period on the left.

2003–2005

Astana International Airport

The project covered design, planning, procurement and construction of Astana International Airport, including all modern mechanical, electrical, electronic and air-navigation systems. Our scope was earthworks and equipment sub-rental.

Client — ALSIM ALARKO
2004–2006

Aktobe — Kostanay border highway

460 km of highway. We carried out 30 km of earthworks and provided equipment sub-rental.

Client — ALSIM ALARKO
2006–2007

Astana wastewater treatment plant

Storm drainage and rainwater removal works within the capital's treatment plant project.

Client — Doğuş Holding (Turkey)
2006–2008

Atyrau — Aktau highway reconstruction

The project covered design, procurement, full construction and reconstruction of 122.6 km of single-carriageway road between Oporny and Beyneu in Mangystau Region. Our scope was earthworks and equipment rental.

Client — ALSIM ALARKO
2007–2008

Tlendiev Street reconstruction, Astana

6 km of urban arterial road. Earthworks and equipment sub-rental in tight city conditions.

Client — Sine Midas Stroy
2007–2015

Water and sewerage, Astana

Eight years of subcontracted earthworks plus subcontracted installation of sewer pipes and potable water pipelines. The longest-running project in the company's history.

Client — ALSIM ALARKO
2014–2016

Astana — Karaganda — Balkhash — Almaty

A section of national highway from km 1416 to 1444. Subcontracted earthworks and equipment rental.

Client — Sine Midas Stroy
2020

BAKAD Ring Road

Taking part in the Big Almaty Ring Road — Central Asia's largest infrastructure PPP project.

Client — ALSIM ALARKO
